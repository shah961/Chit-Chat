A Authentic Culinary Destination

Lahore's Street Food Culture Meets Modern Dining

Located in the vibrant Shahi Mohallah along Fort Road, Chit Chaat offers a welcoming space for families, friends, and late-night food enthusiasts. We bring together iconic street food favorites and classic local recipes prepared with care and fresh ingredients.

Whether you are craving crispy pani puri, rich desi classics, grilled delights, or refreshing drinks in the evening, our doors remain open late into the night.
