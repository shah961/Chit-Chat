Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to the official website of Chit Chaat / Your Local Restaurant. We respect your personal privacy and are committed to maintaining transparent practices regarding any information you share with us while browsing our website.

2. Information We Collect

This website functions primarily as an informational showcase for our restaurant. We collect minimal personal data:

  • Voluntarily Provided Information: When you interact with our website contact form, you may choose to input your name, phone number, email address, or message details.
  • Technical Web Logs: Standard non-identifying technical data (such as browser type, device category, language preferences, and basic server request logs) may be logged automatically by web hosting servers for operational diagnostic purposes.

3. Use of Information

Any information voluntarily submitted via our website form or telephone inquiry is used solely to respond to your specific questions, assist with table or group availability inquiries, or improve our hospitality services.

4. Third-Party Links & External Services

Our website contains links to third-party services such as Instagram (@chitchaatlocal_) and Google Maps for directions. We do not operate or control these third-party websites. When navigating to external links, please review the respective privacy policies of those platforms.

5. Cookies & Tracking

This website does not require active tracking cookies for basic navigation. Standard local web browser caching is used strictly to enhance page loading speed and performance for returning visitors.

6. Security

We implement appropriate technical precautions to safeguard any information submitted through our site. However, please note that transmission over the internet cannot be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

7. Contacting Us Regarding Privacy

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us directly by phone at +92 42 111 124 228 or visit us at 2190-A Fort Road, Shahi Mohallah, Walled City of Lahore, Lahore, Punjab 54110, Pakistan.
