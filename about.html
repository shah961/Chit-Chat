Our Story & Setting

About Chit Chaat

A local restaurant located on Fort Road in Lahore's historic Walled City district.

Who We Are

Authentic Local Dining Experience

Chit Chaat is a local restaurant located on Fort Road in Lahore’s Walled City area, offering a casual dining experience centered around local and Pakistani-inspired food.

Our primary vision is to celebrate the vibrant food heritage of Lahore in a welcoming, clean, and comfortable setting. We pride ourselves on serving freshly prepared street chaats, hearty main courses, crispy sides, and traditional hot and cold beverages.

Our Location

The Spirit of Fort Road & Shahi Mohallah

Situated at 2190-A Fort Road, Shahi Mohallah, Chit Chaat is positioned right in the heart of Lahore’s most famous historical culinary corridor. The Walled City is world-renowned for its passionate relationship with food, hospitality, and vibrant evening life.

Whether you are exploring the heritage sites during sunset or looking for a comforting meal late at night, our restaurant offers an accessible gathering spot for locals and tourists alike.

Guest Experience

What You Can Expect

Fresh Local Preparation

Every dish—from tangy gol gappay to sizzling grilled kebabs—is crafted using quality local ingredients and traditional spicing techniques.

Welcoming Ambiance

A clean, warm, and relaxing environment suitable for families, student groups, and night owls seeking quality meals late into the morning.

Evening to Dawn Hours

Operating daily from 5:00 PM to 5:00 AM, providing reliable food service during crucial evening and late-night hours.
