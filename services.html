Food Showcase

Menu Categories & Offerings

Explore our reported culinary specialities below. Approximate price range: PKR 500–1,000 per person.

Chaat & Street Food

A classic staple of Lahori culture. Prepared fresh with authentic tamarind, mint, and yoghurt chutneys.

  • Pani Puri / Gol Gappay: Crispy hollow puris filled with spiced chickpeas, served with chilled tangy water.
  • Papri Chaat: Crispy papri crisps layered with spiced potatoes, chickpeas, sweetened yoghurt, and chutneys.
  • Dahi Bhallay: Soft lentil dumplings soaked in seasoned yoghurt with sweet and spicy toppings.
  • Samosa Chaat: Golden fried samosas crushed and smothered in chickpeas and tangy house sauces.

Pakistani & Desi Favourites

Rich traditional mains cooked with aromatic local spices and served piping hot.

  • Nihari: Slow-cooked, deeply spiced traditional beef stew garnished with ginger and fresh lime.
  • Grilled Kebabs: Charcoal-grilled minced meat kebabs marinated in traditional local seasoning.
  • Desi Specials: A rotation of regional Pakistani main courses and gravies.

Grills, Pizza & Casual Bites

Modern casual food options ideal for sharing or quick snacking during late-night outings.

  • Grilled Sandwiches: Warm toasted sandwiches filled with savory spiced fillings and melted cheese.
  • Crispy Seasoned Fries: Golden potato fries tossed in aromatic house spices.
  • Fresh Pizzas: Freshly baked pizzas topped with flavorful sauces and cheese.

Drinks & Desserts

Refreshing cold beverages and sweet treats to complete your meal.

  • Traditional Tea & Karak Chai: Freshly brewed aromatic local tea perfect for late evening conversations.
  • Speciality Coffee: Warm brewed coffee options for night owls.
  • Cold Lassi: Traditional chilled yoghurt-based drink, sweet or salted.
  • Cheesecakes & Desserts: Smooth, decadent slices of cheesecake and sweet regional desserts.

Note on Availability & Pricing: Menu items, daily specials, and exact prices are subject to seasonal availability. Please contact the restaurant at +92 42 111 124 228 for today's menu offerings.
